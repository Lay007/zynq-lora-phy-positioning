# FPGA workspace

This directory will contain HDL Coder output, hand-written PL integration, and
verification. Planned top-level areas are `generated/`, `rtl/`, `tb/`,
`constraints/`, `scripts/`, and `ip/`. Generated Vivado products belong under
`fpga/build/` and are ignored.

Algorithmic Verilog is generated from the reviewed Simulink model. Do not patch
generated files to change behavior: update MATLAB requirements or the Simulink
source, regenerate, and rerun cosimulation. Hand-written RTL is reserved for
integration logic such as clock/reset control, board interfaces, and wrappers
that are not appropriate HDL Coder targets.

Every DSP block should define:

- sample format, scaling, rounding, and saturation;
- AXI-Stream framing and backpressure behavior;
- latency and throughput;
- reset and clock-domain assumptions;
- golden vectors generated by the MATLAB floating-point model;
- assertions and boundary-condition tests.

## Current generated output

`model/simulink/run_hdl_generation.m` writes Verilog for the two hardware-bound
DUTs into `generated/`:

- `generated/fft-correlator-fixed/` — 16-bit fixed-point FFT correlator,
  77 Verilog files, 0 HDL errors.
- `generated/joint-timing-cfo/` — joint timing/CFO estimator, 2 Verilog files,
  0 HDL errors.

HDL Coder's traceability HTML, the generated model, and its `.mat` are ignored:
they are large and regenerable. The Verilog is committed so that resource and
interface claims can be audited.

Measured operator counts and the added pipeline latency are published in
[`docs/simulink-m2-acceptance.md`](../docs/simulink-m2-acceptance.md). They are
HDL Coder counts, not synthesis results. Nothing here has been cosimulated or
synthesized: no HDL simulator and no Vivado are installed on the development
host.
